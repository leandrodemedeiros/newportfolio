## Context

Sittax has 6 products for accounting firms and tax professionals. As the only designer, I couldn't design every screen of every product. The answer had to be a system other people could use without me.

## What I did

- **Tokens and components.** I defined color, type and spacing tokens and a library of reusable components, with special attention to the patterns tax software relies on most: forms, navigation and data tables.
- **UI kit.** I built the UI kit in Figma as the single source of truth for design and engineering.
- **Documentation automated with AI.** I use Claude to generate and maintain the system's documentation, so it keeps up with what's in production instead of going stale.

## Why this matters now

With AI, anyone on the team can generate a screen in seconds. Without a documented system, every generated screen invents its own look and the product turns into a patchwork. With tokens, components and written rules, AI generates within the standard, and design review can focus on what matters: the flow, the exceptions and accessibility.
