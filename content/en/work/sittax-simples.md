## Context

Sittax Simples is Sittax's main product. It automates tax calculation, a task accounting firms do every month, for many clients at once and against legal deadlines. The people using it are accountants and their teams: they know the tax rules in depth and have no time to lose with the interface.

I was responsible for the product's UX and UI, from research to delivery, working directly with the business teams.

## The problem

The biggest pain in tax calculation has always come down to three things: seeing the data clearly, checking the numbers and trusting the result before filing. To get there, accountants relied on a large number of spreadsheets to gather invoices and calculate taxes.

The goal of the redesign was to take that work out of spreadsheets. The product now fetches and calculates invoices automatically, suggests adjustments when something doesn't match, and lets accountants issue the payment slip and file the return in one click.

## What I did

- Ran user research: interviews, discovery sessions, data analysis and usability tests with clickable prototypes.
- Reorganized the information architecture: navigation, search, labeling and the product's interface patterns.
- Redesigned the flows and interface, built on the design system I created for the company's 6 products.

## Results

The redesign spanned the period in which Sittax grew from 1,000 to 6,000 customer firms. In 2025, with the new experience of Sittax Simples, its main product, I contributed to a platform that saved customers 255,000 hours of work and avoided R$504 million in improper taxes, according to Sittax.
